    const session = verifyUserSession(readCookie(req, USER_SESSION_COOKIE));

The roster list, existing rep detail, intelligence summary, session drill-down,
and real-conversation review now all call requireManagerOrQaForOffice without
reading requesterId. The existing role and same-office checks are preserved
inside authorizeRosterRequest, but its identity now comes only from the verified
cookie. server/userSession.ts signs/verifies an HMAC cookie containing userId and
expiry; /api/logout clears it, and .env.example documents USER_SESSION_SECRET.

The manager dashboard's stats, command-center extras, widget configuration, and
manager session-detail routes also now derive manager/QA identity from that
verified session rather than requesterId.
